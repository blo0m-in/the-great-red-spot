About Us

Who We Are

We are M. Ossa and J. Taboada — two creatives who believe that the best gifts come from the heart and the hands. Together we build The Great Red Spot, a handmade gift shop born from a shared love for art and meaningful connection.

Our Mission

To tangibilize the love you feel for the people around you. We turn your intentions into objects, poems, and illustrations that speak when words fall short.

Our Vision

A world where every gift tells a real story. We want handmade to become the new standard for meaningful giving — one spot at a time.

Why We Started

We noticed that busy people — people who deeply care — often struggle to find the time and the words to show it. We started The Great Red Spot to close that gap, with our hands.

Our Story

It started with clay, a kitchen table, and a lot of late nights. What began as gifts for friends slowly became something bigger — a real spot where creativity and care collide.

Our Customer

The flavorful busy dreamer. Someone who lives fully, loves deeply, and wants their gifts to reflect that — even when life doesn't leave much time to create.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the ordering process work?

It's simple! Reach out through our contact form or DM us on social media. We'll ask for your prompt or idea, any references you have, and discuss all the details together before we get started. No surprises — just collaboration.

How long does it take to receive my order?

Delivery times vary by product. Speed poetry is usually ready within 24 hours. Illustrations and custom designs take 3–7 days depending on complexity. Clay trinkets may take 1–2 weeks. We'll always give you a timeline before we begin.

Can I request something that isn't listed?

Absolutely — our name is The Great Red Spot, not The Small Fixed Menu. If you have a creative idea for a gift that doesn't fit neatly into our categories, send us a message. We love a good challenge.
